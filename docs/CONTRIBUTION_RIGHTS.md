preparation session. The two historical first-party author identities were

Before merging any externally authored material, record: contribution paths and
commit/PR identifiers; contributor identity privately; original ownership and
employer authorization where applicable; third-party sources and licenses;
executed agreement identifier/date; explicit copyright and patent rights
supporting AGPL and separate commercial distribution; reviewer approval.
Keep executed agreements and signatures private. A public ledger should contain
only consented aliases, contribution identifiers and agreement status.

A DCO sign-off or ordinary AGPL contribution is not a commercial relicensing
grant. An intake policy is not an executed CLA. Have the actual agreement
reviewed before accepting external contributions. If history or a source review
identifies a distinct external copyright claim conflicting with this baseline,
stop the affected publication/merge and report it to the owner.

Issue reports are welcome. Do not submit real profiles, private logs, credentials
or personal databases. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
