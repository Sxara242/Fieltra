# Contribution policy

Public Fieltra code is licensed under **AGPL-3.0-only**. The owner
intends to offer separate commercial licenses for code for which they hold
sufficient rights. Third-party libraries retain their own licenses.

External code contributions are **not accepted for merging yet**. Before any
external contribution is merged, the owner and contributor must have a written
agreement granting the owner sufficient copyright and patent rights to offer
the contribution under both AGPL-3.0-only and separate commercial terms.
The contributor must identify third-party material and have authority to grant
those rights, including employer permission where applicable.

AGPL licensing alone, a pull request, or a DCO sign-off does not grant commercial
relicensing rights. This document is a contribution intake policy, not an
executed CLA and not a commercial license. Obtain legal review of the actual
agreement and record acceptance before opening contribution intake. The same
rights review applies to any existing externally authored code.

Bug reports and discussion are welcome; do not include personal databases,
backups, credentials, tokens or private logs.

The existing rights baseline was confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-09. See
[docs/CONTRIBUTION_RIGHTS.md](docs/CONTRIBUTION_RIGHTS.md) for the attestation,
scope and future ledger. This policy covers code, documentation, translations
and artwork. Keep executed agreements private. Do not merge external patches
until the agreement is executed and recorded.

Follow [docs/BUILDING.md](docs/BUILDING.md) for development and isolated tests.
Windows is the current release target; Android remains in development.
Shared changes must consider both clients.
